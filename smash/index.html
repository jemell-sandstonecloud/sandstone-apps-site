Do $800 of damage. You have 45 seconds.

A one-thumb game for iPhone and Android. Each job gives you a room to wreck, with a dollar target and a clock. Swing through it and throw things at other things until you beat the number.

How a round goes

One thing in the room has a yellow KEEP tag. Throw it in the box by the door and your heat doubles for 10 seconds. Break it and you can't get the third star that round.

With 10 seconds left a Big One rolls in, like a fridge or a copier, and you try to take it down before the buzzer.

Then a cartoon target walks in, depending on what you picked before the round: The Boss, The Ticket, The Folks or The Ex. You get about 8 seconds to knock them over. It's slapstick, with no blood or injury. After that a handwritten bill prints, listing what you broke.

What there is to get

There are four rooms and 12 jobs, with 36 stars to get. Spend what you did on more things to break and on gear, like a frying pan or a sledgehammer. The dollars are play money. Every price is shown before you buy.

What isn't here

There are no ads and no purchases. You don't need an account and nothing leaves your phone. Uninstalling removes everything. The privacy page has the details.
