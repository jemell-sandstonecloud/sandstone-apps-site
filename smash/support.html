Privacy Support

Support

Write to support@sandstonecloud.com. Tell us your phone model and the version number at the bottom of Settings, plus what you were doing when it went wrong. The game can't send us anything itself, so your email is all we'll see.

Questions we expect

Do I need an account?

No. The game opens right on the first job.

Does it cost anything?

No. There are no ads and no purchases. The dollars in the game are play money.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Plan on starting fresh. The save stays on the phone it was made on and isn't in any cloud backup. A phone-to-phone transfer tool might copy it across, but we can't promise that.

How do I start over?

Go to Settings and choose Reset progress. Tap it twice, then confirm. Your settings stay.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game, and everything goes with it. We don't hold anything, so there's nothing to request.

Why won't it take the name I typed?

Names can use letters, numbers and spaces, 14 characters at most. A filter blocks profanity, slurs and a short list of public figures, and the target goes by its role instead. If it blocked something it shouldn't have, tell us.

The haptics or the motion are too much.

Settings has haptics at Off, Light or Full, a sound switch and reduced motion. Reduced motion follows your phone's setting until you change it.

I keep grabbing things by accident.

Change Grab delay in Settings from 200 ms to 300 ms.

I'm left-handed.

Turn on Left-hand mode in Settings. It moves the heat badge and the pause button to the other side.
