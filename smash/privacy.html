Privacy Support

Privacy policy

Last updated November 2, 2026.

What's the Damage is made by Sandstone Cloud Solutions LLC. The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. There's no account and no sign-in. The game has no ads and no analytics, and it makes no network calls. We don't collect, receive, sell or share anything about you, and we have no way to see how you play.

What stays on your phone

The game keeps one save file on your phone, and it holds:

  • your stars, best scores, money, stock, gear and levels
  • story progress, damage ledger, settings and how many times it has asked for a store review
  • how you dressed the figures, and any names you typed for them

The save is kept out of iCloud and computer backups on iPhone, and Android's app backup is turned off, so the file doesn't go to any cloud backup. We can't see it. A phone-to-phone transfer tool might copy it across, but don't count on that.

Names

You can name the cartoon targets and your own figure. Those names stay in the save file. A filter blocks profanity, slurs and a short list of public figures, and it runs on the phone. There are no photo uploads.

Sharing a bill

If you tap Share, your phone's share sheet opens with a picture of your bill, and you choose where it goes. Nothing is sent unless you send it. A copy of the picture can sit in the game's temporary folder on your phone until the next share or until you uninstall. The picture shows the role, like The Boss, and never a name you typed. On iPhone, the game asks to add to Photos only if you choose Save Image, and it can't read your photos.

Links in the game

Settings has links to this page and the support page. They open in your browser, and that's the only thing in the game that goes online. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log a visit the way any web host does. The pages set no cookies and run no scripts.

Apple and Google

Apple and Google run the stores. They may show us their usual download numbers, and crash reports from people who agreed to share them in their phone's settings. That comes from them and is covered by their policies. The game doesn't send it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes everything. To start over without uninstalling, go to Settings and choose Reset progress. There's no account, so there's nothing else to delete and no deletion request to make.

Age

This game is made for adults and isn't directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If the game ever changes what it does with data, this page will change first, with a new date at the top.
