Word Doors Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-13. Word Doors is made by Sandstone Cloud Solutions LLC.

The short version

Word Doors has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your progress stays on your phone. The app sends nothing about you to us.

What Word Doors keeps

  • Your progress, your settings and what you have unlocked. These are kept on your phone only.
  • Nothing is kept on a server of ours, because Word Doors doesn't send anything to one.

What we receive

Nothing from the app itself. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and we use them only to answer you.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We store no payment details and never see your card. Your phone keeps a record of what you unlocked, and Settings has Restore purchases for a new phone. Tips don't unlock anything.

What Apple and Google receive

Apple or Google may share crash reports and download counts with us if you allowed that on your phone. These reports don't name you. Their own privacy policies cover what they collect.

How to delete your data

Delete Word Doors from your phone. That removes everything the app kept on your phone. The data rights page has the rest.

Children

Word Doors is not made for children and we don't knowingly collect anything from a child.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new effective date.
