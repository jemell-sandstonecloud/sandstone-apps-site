Common questions

Where is my progress kept?

On your phone only. Word Doors has no account and no server copy, so we can't see it or move it to another phone.

I got a new phone. Can I move my progress?

No. Progress stays on the phone it was made on.

Purchases come with you: open Settings on the new phone and tap Restore purchases.

How do I delete everything?

Delete Word Doors from your phone. The data rights page and our privacy policy say more.

What if something looks wrong?

Email support@sandstonecloud.com with your phone model and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
