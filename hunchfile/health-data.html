Consumer Health Data Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy is for people in Washington. It adds to our privacy policy.

Hunchfile isn't built for health information and doesn't ask for it. A word check on the phone keeps most notes that look like health info off our server. It looks for certain words and can miss things, so a note with a health detail could still reach our server if memory is on.

What we could collect: the words of a note, written by a Hunchfile user, that happens to mention someone's health, such as a condition, a symptom, a treatment or a medication.

Why: to store and show that note to the person who wrote it, to find earlier notes that look related for that person, and to answer rights requests. Apart from the word check on the phone, which sends nothing, we don't work out anything about anyone's health from notes, and we never sell this data.

Where it comes from: the person writing the note.

Who can receive it: Amazon Web Services, which hosts our server, and Amazon Bedrock, an AWS service that runs AI models, including Amazon's and Anthropic's, as our service providers. We have no affiliates that receive it.

Your rights: you can ask whether we hold health data about you, see it, withdraw consent and have it deleted. Users can do most of this in the app. Anyone, user or not, can email support@sandstonecloud.com. We never look through notes by name or by words, so we may not be able to find or confirm a note. We'll answer within 30 days. If we say no, you can appeal by replying to that email.
