Delete your Hunchfile data

In the app: Settings, Delete everything, then confirm. It removes your notes from your phone, and your notes and memory from our server. The app shows you the status. If it can't finish, it says so and keeps trying.

If you no longer have the app: email support@sandstonecloud.com with the subject "Delete my Hunchfile data" and your user ID. You'll find it in Settings, under Your user ID, and at the top of any export file. We'll reply within 30 days. Without the ID we can't find your account, because there's no sign-in and we never look through notes by name or by words.

Deleting the app from your phone removes what's on your phone. It doesn't remove what's on our server. If an account has no activity for 180 days, we delete what's on our server anyway.

What's left afterward: a deletion record with ids and a time and no note text, server logs for up to 90 days, and backups for up to 14 days. Delete everything can't reach a backup your phone has already made.
