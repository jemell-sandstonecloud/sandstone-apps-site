Hunchfile Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Hunchfile is made by Sandstone Cloud Solutions LLC ("we"). This policy explains what leaves your phone, where it goes, and how to remove it.

The short version

Your notes stay off our server unless you turn on memory. If you turn it on, your notes go to our server and can also be sent to Amazon Bedrock, an AWS service that runs AI models, including Amazon's and Anthropic's. Right now our own server picks the earlier notes and nothing goes to Amazon Bedrock. We may change that later, and if we do we'll update this page first. We don't show ads and we don't sell your data. Your notes are not used to train large language models.

What's on your phone

Your notes, threads, the sample notes, your birth year and your settings. Hunchfile makes no network requests until you agree to memory. Your phone's own backup may copy your notes, under Apple's or Google's terms. Notes that look like health info or a password don't go to our server, even with memory on, and we mark them to be left out of device backups. That check looks for certain words on your phone and can miss things. Our server runs the same word check too and stores nothing when it finds something.

What leaves your phone if you turn on memory

  • Your notes, thread names and any fixes you make to memory. Dates you wrote or chose go with them. Links are removed from notes before they reach memory.
  • A random user ID we create for this install. It isn't your name, email, Apple ID or Google account.
  • A record that you said yes to the AI and memory questions, that you said you're an adult, and when. Your birth year isn't sent.
  • If you report an earlier note: which one, and the reason you picked. Never note text.

Who gets it

  • Sandstone Cloud Solutions LLC runs the server and the memory service. Our server runs on Amazon Web Services in the United States. AWS is bound by contract to use your data only to provide its services to us, keep it confidential and secure, and delete it when we ask.
  • Amazon Bedrock can receive note text to read and match it, if we change the server method as described above.
  • We don't send note text to a web search service or to anyone else.

Other people in your notes

Notes can name people who don't use Hunchfile. Those names and dates are stored on our server with the note, and names can go to Amazon Bedrock. Please leave out what they wouldn't want shared. If you think a Hunchfile note is about you, email us. We never look through notes by name or by words. We'll reply in writing with what we can and can't do, and how to appeal.

Testing what works

Later versions may record which earlier notes you tap or hide, to compare ways of picking them. This version doesn't. Those records would hold ids and times, never note text, and we'd update this page first. The app has no analytics or advertising software.

Health information

Hunchfile isn't built for health information, so please don't use it for that. See our consumer health data policies.

How long we keep it

Your notes and memory stay on our server until you delete them, delete everything or turn memory off. Turning memory off deletes the server copy within 15 days. If an account has no activity for 180 days, we delete what's on our server. Reports are kept for 90 days or until the account is deleted. Server logs are kept for 90 days, and they hold the time and the request, never note text or your IP address. Backups expire within 14 days.

Deleting

  • Delete a note: this removes it from your phone and our server.
  • Delete a memory: this removes our copy and leaves your note on your phone.
  • Turn memory off: this deletes our copy within 15 days and leaves your notes on your phone.
  • Delete everything: Settings, Delete everything. This removes your notes from your phone, and your notes and memory from our server. The app shows you the status. If it can't finish, it says so and keeps trying.

What's left afterward: a deletion record with ids and a time and no note text, the 90-day server logs, and backups until they expire. Delete everything can't reach a backup your phone has already made. You can remove those in your phone's settings.

Without the app, email us your user ID. It's in Settings and at the top of any export file. Reinstalling on Android makes a new account, and the old one is deleted after 180 days of no use.

Export

Settings, Export gives you one Markdown file with your notes and, if memory was ever on, what's on our server.

Age

Hunchfile is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If the app locks because someone is under 18, we delete what's on our server. If you think someone under 18 has used it, email us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete data about you, and to withdraw consent. You can do most of that in the app, and anything else by email. You can turn off memory any time in Settings. If we say no to a request, you can appeal by replying to that email. If you're still not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date. If we want to use your notes in a new way, we'll ask first.
